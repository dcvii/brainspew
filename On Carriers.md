With the USS Abraham Lincoln still making headlines, this week felt like the right moment to step back and look at the carrier itself — what it takes to command one, what life aboard actually looks like, and what all those colored jerseys on the flight deck mean.

Vice Admiral Ted “Slapshot” Carter walks through what it takes to earn command of a nuclear-powered carrier, from the career path to the grueling training track that every prospective CO has to survive. Then Mooch and Hozer take you aboard USS Harry S. Truman (CVN-75) operating off Florida for an up-close look at life and work on a supercarrier. And Chester Hull, author of the coffee table book “Life on the Flight Deck,” breaks down the color-coded jersey system that keeps organized chaos from becoming just chaos on a Nimitz class flight deck. Three episodes that put the Lincoln story in context.
