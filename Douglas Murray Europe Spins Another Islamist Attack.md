The suspect in Berlin’s recent rampage was known to authorities. He slipped through the cracks because leaders minimize the threat of radical Islam.

By now it’s all starting to sound like a bad joke. An Islamist terrorist drives a van into a Berlin Pride parade, killing one person and injuring dozens more. And how is it reported? Much of the media went with the usual formulation: that the attacker might as well have been a self-automated vehicle. The white van “didn’t exactly hit the parade,” one typical report went, but as it drove through the parade it “attacked the people who were going away from the parade.”

As I have had cause to say too many times, this is something which, from Stockholm to Nice, is best understood as the “naughty van” phenomenon. If a van or car is driven into a crowd by an Islamist, you can swiftly tell who the culprit is because the vehicle will be talked about as though it suddenly took on a life of its own and began a rampage.

The reality is even more repetitive. The perpetrator of Saturday’s attack was a 21-year-old called [Abdul Ballout](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/berlin-germany-pride-attack-abdul-ballout-isis/). Born in Germany, of Lebanese parentage, last year he reportedly went to Lebanon to try to get to Syria in order to join ISIS. Arrested in Lebanon, he served [three months](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c62eg899l99o) in prison for a range of offenses including incitement to religious and sectarian conflict.

On returning to Germany he was convicted of preparing a serious act of violence and endangering the state, among other charges, and was sentenced to [less than two years](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/berlin-lgbtq-pride-christopher-street-attack-ramming-suspect-search-germany/) in prison. However the court seems to have released him, and he was put into a course of “ [deradicalization](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/berlin-germany-pride-attack-abdul-ballout-isis/).” He apparently went to two meetings. His third meeting with the Berlin-based Violence Prevention Network was meant to take place Monday.

At this point, everyone in Europe knows which positions to assume. The organizers of the Pride parade gave a press conference in which they admitted that they had [hoped the perpetrator](https://x.com/visegrad24/status/2081502495684087877?s=20) had been a white Christian man. Isn’t it disappointing when the world doesn’t cohere with your desires?

Across the political landscape and mainstream media in Europe, you can hear the same pining for a more politically convenient reality. A British Labour MP, Kate Osborne, took to social media to respond to the attack. You may have thought, as a lesbian herself, she had some sense of where things are going when Pride parades, like Christmas markets, have to be all bollarded up. But Osborne has no hint of that, or even apparently much survival instinct. She responded to the Berlin attack by depicting it as part of a general rise of anti-LGBTQ rhetoric. She blamed the trend on the anti-immigration Reform Party in the UK and also GB News, a right-leaning broadcaster. (For what it’s worth, Reform and GB News are so packed with gays that I sometimes watch and wonder whether the straights shouldn’t be able to get a presenting slot every now and then.)

But identifying the real source of the problem ought to be the easy part. It’s the first step toward tackling the much greater problem that is ensnaring Britain, Europe, and to a lesser extent, the U.S.

By opening Western borders to Third World immigration for decades, generations of politicians allowed in people who had completely different standards and values from the societies into which they had moved. Beginning with Germany’s *[Gastarbeiter](https://www.britannica.com/money/guest-worker)* program in the 1950s, it was just presumed that the migrants—including from Muslim countries—would either leave after a time or settle and become as European as anyone else.

Of course, neither of these things happened. And it took until the 2010s for Europe’s leaders even to admit that nothing to do with postwar migration had gone according to plan. But by then Europe was faced with a problem it had no answer for: How do you integrate a community that doesn’t want to integrate? How do you stop people from hating the society that has welcomed them?

In Europe the answer is that you tinker. You come up—for instance—with “deradicalization programs” and a whole new boondoggle emerges, whereby so-called experts across Europe suggest to the government that they can step in at any moment and “deradicalize” people. We have seen many times before how unsuccessful these programs can be—not least with the [second London Bridge attack](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/video/2019/nov/29/how-the-london-bridge-terror-attack-unfolded-video-report) in 2019, when [Usman Khan](https://www.counterextremism.com/extremists/usman-khan), a former rehab subject, was invited to address a conference and proceeded to stab three people. The programs put a scientific veneer on a very unscientific problem. And so the problem continues.

As though to demonstrate this point, two days after the attack in Berlin, a man wielding a knife and screaming “Allahu Akbar” [stabbed three women](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/paris-stabbing-knife-attack-clichy/) —including one who is pregnant—in Porte de Clichy in Paris. If anyone is new to all of this they might note that the “Allahu Akbar” is the same cry that was heard on the Upper West Side of Manhattan a few days ago when a man started [stabbing at Jews](https://abc7ny.com/post/suspect-pair-upper-west-side-stabbings-expected-face-charges-leaders-call-hate-crime-prosecution/19567357/).

For those of us who have covered this story for years, none of it is shocking.

It is to be expected that Islamist terrorists will want to attack Christmas markets, pop concerts, Pride parades, and much more. It is to be expected that the media and many politicians will continue to try to put off the day that they actually acknowledge the problem that is facing them.

But what remains a marvel to some of us is that the moment of “waking up” never really comes. To put it another way, some people are so keen to remain in their delusion that they will deny the truth even as the weaponized vehicle accelerates toward them.

In the aftermath of Saturday’s attack, late in the evening, a reporter for the German broadcaster NTV [told viewers](https://www-danisch-de.translate.goog/blog/2026/07/26/die-queere-community-fuehlt-sich-natuerlich-vor-allen-dingen-von-rechten-kraeften-bedroht/?_x_tr_sl=de&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=en&_x_tr_pto=wapp) watching live that “The queer community naturally feels threatened, above all, by right-wing forces.” By that stage in the day the perpetrator’s identity was known. His motives could be easily guessed at. But somehow it doesn’t fit to say that the queer community feels threatened by Islamists. It sounds as though different stripes of the great rainbow coalition might be “othering” each other. Perhaps it isn’t very “accepting” to identify the Islamists.

So as in New York and Paris and too many other cities to name, the usual formula will be adopted. For the foreseeable future we must hope that all hate is carried out by white Christian men. We must hope that no Muslim extremists ever commit acts of violence, or that if they do, they are provably mentally ill. We must hope that we all get along. And we must hope that “love” conquers “hate.”

As long-term readers will know, I’m not sure that any of this is going to work. *The Sunday Times* reported that the Berlin attacker was a known member of Berlin’s “Islamist scene.” That was a new one to me, but there we have it: the modern West in a nutshell. Berlin has many great scenes. If you like dance music you can be part of Berlin’s vibrant “dance music scene.” If being gay is your thing then you could be part of Berlin’s wild and proud “gay scene.” And if you don’t like either of the above then you might decide to slide into Berlin’s out and proud “Islamist scene.” The modern West pretends all these “scenes” can get along together. My fear is that in the end one of them will win out.
