*Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu speaking to the United Nations General Assembly Sep. 24:*

Mr. \[Zohran\] Mamdani, since you were elected mayor of this city, many Jews no longer feel safe in New York. They talk to me. They tell me. This isn’t the city we remember. It changed so quickly. Now it’s no wonder. You praise criminals convicted of supporting [Hamas](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/hamas). You count as friends people like Hasan Piker, who said America deserved 9-11. America deserved 9-11....

Mr. Mamdani, you try to stop me from coming here. You try to silence me. Well, you can’t silence me.

You can’t silence the truth. And here’s the simple truth. Here’s the simple truth. [Israel](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/israel) didn’t commit genocide. Israel prevented genocide. And that’s exactly, that’s exactly what Mr. Mamdani’s Hamas buddies would have done to us had we not stopped them. They would have killed every last one of us. The lies you and other antisemites spread about the Jews and the Jewish state have real consequences. In New York, Jews are threatened on the way to synagogue.
