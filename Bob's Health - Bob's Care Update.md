## Revised Hospice vs Palliative Distinction

While medical guidelines often define **hospice care** as appropriate for those with **six months or less to live** and a decision to **forego curative treatment**, **many families rightly understand hospice more broadly** as:

- A **coordinated model of care** for older adults with **debilitating or complex chronic conditions** (not just cancer or actively dying).
- A system that brings in **consistent, high-touch support**: nurses, aides, social workers, chaplains, and sometimes volunteers.
- **Not limited to pain management** —instead, focused on **comfort, safety, and family relief**, especially when the patient’s decline demands 24-hour readiness and clinical oversight, but not hospital-level intervention.

This is especially important when **family caregivers are overwhelmed**, and the focus shifts to **maintaining dignity, stability, and peace** in the home setting.
