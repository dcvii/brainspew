Two stories this week that cut to the core of what’s straining naval aviation right now.

Congressman Rob Wittman joins Mooch at NAS Oceana to talk through the full list: The F/A-XX schedule, Ford Class construction, operational tempo, deployment lengths, and the mid-career retention crisis, from the perspective of someone on the Hill who has to fund and oversee all of it.

Then Hozer is back with a story that deserves far more attention than it’s getting: a government report accusing the Navy of ignoring medical studies linking cumulative G-force exposure over careers of carrier operations to brain injuries — the same injuries being connected to Super Hornet pilot suicides. Mooch and Hozer dig into who in the Navy owns this problem, and whether the culture of mental health stigma is keeping aviators from coming forward before it’s too late.

The encore is a good one to pair with both stories. Jell-O Aiello — host of the Fighter Pilot Podcast — traces his path from a teenager with a dream to the front seat of an F/A-18 flying off carriers in harm’s way. The kind of story that reminds you exactly who the retention crisis and the TBI problem are happening to.
