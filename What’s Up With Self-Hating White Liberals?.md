[Zach Goldberg](https://open.substack.com/users/17347997-zach-goldberg?utm_source=mentions) ’s most famous graph depicts the strange phenomenon whereby every racial group in America likes themselves, except for White liberals, who like other groups more than they like Whites.

![The American White Savior Complex - Tablet Magazine](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zxQ8!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4370e070-38a6-4aeb-8af2-b8345d5b5d66_2560x1138.jpeg)

The American White Savior Complex - Tablet Magazine

I’ve seen this graph enough times that I got curious about how well it replicated, and what else we could learn from this sort of data. So, I grabbed the data and set about answering some questions.

**#1**: *Does it replicate?* **Yes.** The plot itself holds up in adjacent waves of the same survey.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!13KM!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F199d34ee-df30-4e98-b04a-41a08364f555_3480x2070.png)

**#2**: *Does a self-derogating split across ideological lines extend to other races?* **No.** Other races consistently like themselves, regardless of their politics. It’s just White liberals.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Rbk4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F48dfee3d-e0a1-455e-b9c5-7023efd75d40_3360x2070.png)

**#3**: *Has it always been this way?* **No.** White liberal self-derogation is a recent phenomenon, timed after the so-called ‘Great Awokening’ that came with Obama’s re-election and fomenting of race relations.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!g0Zg!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F748ae63c-23ba-459c-a51f-2fe96a37c3f4_3300x2070.png)

**#4**: *Do White conservatives show an opposing trajectory shift?* **No.** White conservatives are about as distinct from one another as they’ve always been. They even seem to have started going in the direction of their liberal counterparts, with weak evidence that such a shift has been larger for the less conservative among them.

**#5:** *Do White liberals show a dose-response?* **Yes.** More liberal Whites show greater racial outgroup preference.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zlUX!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff243cd8c-9843-432a-bac9-cc7073e6cd18_3600x2070.png)

**#6:** *Do the results for ideology transfer to party?* **Yes and no.** The more liberal party does show the same trajectory as liberals more generally.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9vBa!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1a3d1229-685e-46b7-8834-66d9d76bb45b_3360x2070.png)

The ‘ **No** ’ has to do with the dose-response: the split among Democrats is about ideology, not party attachment, and party attachment and ideology are only weakly correlated (*r* = ). As a result, we get this quite different-looking graph:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vRlH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F732a66ef-d27d-41ce-ab60-1817e8102d5f_3600x2070.png)

**#7:** *Has this happened before?* **Not in recorded data.** If we use the older ANES data going back to 1964, we don’t see this happen any other time. We also see that Blacks have reverted from the trajectory other groups were on, where they were heading towards having less ingroup bias over time. They are back to 1960s levels.[^1]

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!gnk5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F043fbd36-8dba-41cd-9ee1-eed24e50a9ce_3480x2070.png)

**#8:** *Is this because White liberals began liking Whites less or liking non-Whites more?* **Both.**

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GpCw!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbb544f26-569c-4358-a6da-644a07c314fc_3420x2070.png)

**#9:** *Does this replicate in other U.S. datasets?* **Yes.** This seems to replicate whenever we have representatively-sampled data from the U.S., in which participants are asked a substantially similar question. For example, here’s alternative ANES results, the VOTER survey, and mention of two additional sources and one dead-end.[^2]

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UzQO!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbf696200-f185-473c-b145-a3a82b00d891_3480x2070.png)

**#10:** *Is this responsive to events?* **Yes.** We generally do not have granular enough data to see whether particular events drive this reporting, but we do for one event: George Floyd’s death. We have weekly polling from the time, and right after it happened, White liberals’ anti-ingroup bias doubled. White moderates were affected in the same way to a smaller extent, whereas conservatives were unaffected.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!gsBp!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd87f61e2-b859-4967-8cdb-0ef26452803b_3480x2070.png)

**#11:** *Did the Floyd effect on warmth affect members of other races?* **Yes.** From the same dataset:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!u8go!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6070d72f-bec2-4904-a9c6-acbc57370820_3180x2070.png)

**#12:** *Does White liberal outgroup preference replicate in international datasets?* **No.** Across Europe, Australia, Canada, etc., attitudes have trended in the same direction, but they have not become as severe as in America: on net, Whites abroad still tend to prefer their own national and racial ingroups, even if said Whites lean left. We see this replicate in the European Social Survey, Australian Election Study, the British Election Study, and so on. I’ll show two charts to make some points.

First, compare the European Anglosphere to the Continent in terms of views on other-race versus same-race immigrants. The preference for own-race immigrants is smaller in the Anglosphere:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!xiwi!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa29fa1aa-c53d-4689-a535-fdad2c64674d_3480x1900.png)

Second, in Britain, the post-Brexit trajectories on immigrant attitudes are curious:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5Byq!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5844ba90-c362-4cbc-bd2a-8e222f6bf615_3480x2070.png)

And I’ll toss in a third chart to make a contrary point: where America, Europe, and the rest of the Anglosphere *differ*: the various in-group attitude measures are most differentiated by politics in the non-U.S. Anglosphere, despite how different the patterns look.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!uuVN!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbb827930-8abe-42ee-ba37-a7ee65f90b93_3300x2070.png)

**Bonus:** *The Great Awokening had a lot of focus on Black-White relations. How do different groups feel about Whites relative to Blacks?* **Outside of non-liberal Whites, everyone now prefers Blacks,** at least in polling.[^3]

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Iu1_!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbfc64441-1354-443f-ac75-ea6dc0543254_3480x2070.png)
