LONDON—Russell Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison following his [conviction for rape and sexual assault](https://www.wsj.com/world/uk/russell-brand-guilty-verdict-02e0ad15?mod=article_inline), a fall from grace for one of the U.K.’s most prominent entertainment figures.

Passing sentence at [London](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/london) ’s Southwark Crown Court Monday, Justice Joel Bennathan described Brand as a “sexual predator” and accused him of “staggering arrogance.”

“You thought fame meant you could act how you liked, when you liked, unhindered by the rules by which decent people live,” he said.

A jury found Brand, 51 years old, guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault on Thursday, following a four-week trial. He must serve two-thirds of the sentence before being eligible for parole, Justice Bennathan said.

Brand, who had pleaded not guilty and consistently denied the allegations, was found not guilty on three other counts, including two charges of rape and one of indecent assault.

The sentencing marks the culmination of an inquiry launched in 2023 by London’s Metropolitan Police during which they interviewed more than 100 witnesses and reviewed thousands of documents after a joint investigation by the Times of London, the Sunday Times and Channel 4 detailed allegations of sexual misconduct and abuse against the entertainer.

Following Thursday’s guilty verdict, the chief of London’s Metropolitan Police said he would not be surprised if “many more” alleged victims came forward.

“There’s two more rape allegations that we’re looking at,” Mark Rowley said. “We’re encouraging anybody else to come forward if there’s any other allegations.”

Speaking outside the court Monday, the lead detective in the case, Andy Furphy, paid tribute to the women who gave evidence. “I hope they recognize just how powerful their actions have been and the difference they may make to others,” he said.

“Reporting sexual offenses can be incredibly difficult and they have shown immense bravery, dignity, and resilience throughout what has been a lengthy and challenging trial process.”

A lawyer representing Brand didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.

Brand built an international profile in the 2000s as an eccentric stand-up comic, radio host and Hollywood actor, starring in films such as 2008’s “Forgetting Sarah Marshall” and 2010’s “Despicable Me.” In recent years, he pivoted away from mainstream entertainment to build an online-media following on platforms such as YouTube, where he amassed millions of subscribers discussing Christianity, right-wing conspiracy and populist causes.

Brand framed the criminal accusations as a coordinated smear campaign orchestrated by legacy media outlets, contending he was being targeted for his antiestablishment political views. Following the initial allegations in 2023, corporate partners and publishers severed ties with him, and YouTube suspended monetization on his channel.
