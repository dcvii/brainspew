Anyone who has seen footage of migrants storming the Spanish enclave of Ceuta knows what will come next.

On Thursday, shocking footage spread online of droves of primarily young male Moroccans storming Ceuta, a small Spanish territory on the North African coast. Thousands of them swam around breakwaters and climbed fences to enter the tiny enclave. An estimated 60,000 people are believed to have arrived in Ceuta—where the permanent population is 84,000—in 24 hours. Eighteen immigrants have died during the crossings. Meanwhile, local bars and shops [are closed](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20210520-panic-pity-and-anger-in-spain-s-ceuta-over-migrant-wave) for fear of looting. The chaos has caused the leader of Ceuta to appeal to Madrid to send in the army.

The Spanish government [has done so](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/spain-deploys-military-ceuta-morocco-border-migrants/), but it should never have come to this. It was bound to, though, because Spanish prime minister Pedro Sánchez, a Socialist, oversees one of Europe’s most permissive migration regimes. In January, Sánchez announced a blanket amnesty program to regularize the immigration status of half a million migrants living illegally in Spain. In a *[New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/04/opinion/spain-migrants-europe.html)* [op-ed](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/04/opinion/spain-migrants-europe.html) published in February, Sánchez characterized the move as both humane and economically necessary, and contrasted his policy to the restrictive ones of “MAGA-style leaders” (read: Hungary’s former prime minister Viktor Orbán).

By summer, applications had [soared to over one million](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/30/world/europe/spain-migrants-legal-status.html) —this, in a country whose migrant population is estimated to be at around 15 percent of the national total. Granted, the beneficiaries of this program are mostly Latin Americans, who, owing to a shared language and cultural similarities, are relatively easy to integrate.

But it also benefits illegal migrants who came to Spain in the same way as the throngs that stormed the Ceuta beaches. Add to this a [recent ruling](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-07-30/el-supremo-determino-que-las-devoluciones-en-caliente-no-se-pueden-aplicar-a-quienes-entren-a-nado-en-ceuta-y-melilla.html) from Spain’s supreme court banning the government from returning migrants intercepted as they try to swim to the autonomous enclaves of Ceuta and Melilla.

Across Europe and the United Kingdom, migrants like this—from Islamic countries and Africa—are overwhelming cities, bringing with them serious social disruption and crime. Sweden has recently [begun to reform](https://unherd.com/newsroom/swedens-immigration-crackdown-is-a-lesson-for-europe/?edition=us) its infamously permissive migration policies after people of migrant backgrounds were shown to be 2.5 times more likely to be criminal than native Swedes.In Germany, [police statistics](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/04/syrians-and-afghans-in-germany-eight-times-as-likely-to-be-suspects-of-violent-crime-as-locals/) released this past April showed that migrants of Syrian and Afghan background are eight to nine times more likely than the average German to be suspects in criminal cases.

Significant [public figures like David Betz](https://www.thefp.com/p/has-an-english-civil-war-already), a scholar in the War Studies department of King’s College London, and Thibault de Montbrial, a senior lawyer who prosecutes Islamist criminals for the French government, have warned that the accelerating crisis pushed Europe closer to civil war. It’s not simply a matter of tension between migrants and natives, but also fury from native Europeans at their governments for permitting the invasion. The horrifying summer blockbuster *Citizen Vigilante*, [reviewed by](https://www.thefp.com/p/douglas-murray-citizen-vigilante-migration) *[The Free Press](https://www.thefp.com/p/douglas-murray-citizen-vigilante-migration)* [’s Douglas Murray](https://www.thefp.com/p/douglas-murray-citizen-vigilante-migration), depicts one scenario Betz has predicted if governments don’t change course.

None of this seems to matter to the European governing class in politics, law, media, academia, and even the Catholic Church. To them, migrants are, as Pope Leo XIV, has described them, “ [privileged witnesses of hope](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2025-07/pope-leo-message-world-day-of-migrants-and-refugees.html).” And shame on anybody who says otherwise.

Criticism of mass migration is treated as taboo, and sometimes as criminal. In Britain, Lucy Connolly received a [31-month jail sentence](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5yl7p4l11po) for a racially inflammatory message on X she posted after receiving news of the Southport stabbings. Connolly deleted it three and a half hours later, but the government still tried, convicted, and sentenced her. The 42-year-old’s case became a political rallying point for people accusing the UK government of practicing “two-tier justice”—that is, holding white Britons to higher standards than non-whites or migrants.

While domestic politicians silence dissent, European Union officials in Brussels, as well as judges in most EU countries, make it all but impossible for national leaders trying to stop the illegal migrant flow to do so. When I asked Italian sources why the right-wing Giorgia Meloni government has made so little progress on this front, they all said that the progressive judiciary consistently ties the hands of elected officials.

If this all sounds like the plot of a dystopian horror novel, well, it is. The late French writer Jean Raspail’s controversial 1973 novel, *[The Camp of the Saints](https://bookshop.org/a/93116/9798988739999),* foresaw exactly the kind of thing happening this week in Ceuta.

In Raspail’s story, the true villains are not the bedraggled migrant armies on the beaches of France, but the ruling-class elites who welcome them as saviors from the psychological burden of being Western. Raspail, who was a traditionalist Catholic, reserved his greatest contempt for churchmen, who, in his telling, are sentimental humanitarians who neither see nor comprehend that the migrant waves will smash what remains of the Church in a de-Christianized Europe.

As Nathan Pinkoski, a young Canadian scholar, writes in the introduction to a recent English-language edition of *[The Camp of the Saints](https://bookshop.org/a/93116/9798988739999)*, the novel explores “the consequences of white guilt.” [According to Pinkoski](https://lyceumnow.com/the-wests-search-for-a-foreign-savior/):

> *This feeling drives the redistribution of wealth and people. Every time Westerners learn of the suffering of non-Westerners, they regard it as their fault and their responsibility. Love of one’s neighbor becomes an unlimited duty to distant strangers. Fulfilling these duties promises to purify the corrupt, tainted West. This passion for the stranger is paired with disdain for one’s actual neighbors. It turns into contempt for one’s own people and, eventually, for oneself. The point is not that these characters love humanity too much. It is that they have learned to call self-hatred love.*

French novelist Michel Houellebecq followed the same thematic model in his controversial 2015 novel *[Submission](https://bookshop.org/a/93116/9781250097347)*, which takes place as an Islamist party comes to power in France peacefully and democratically. Though the book was ignorantly [denounced as Islamophobic](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2015/sep/06/michel-houellebecq-submission-am-i-islamophobic-probably-yes) (and inspired a cartoon on the cover of *Charlie Hebdo* released the day [Islamist militants massacred](https://time.com/3657867/charlie-hebdo-paris-michel-houellebecq/) much of the staff of the newspaper), the book’s villains are, in fact, the decadent French elites, who are too spiritually and morally exhausted to manage their society.

If this all sounds like the plot of a dystopian horror novel, well, it is.

For confirmation of Raspail’s and Houellebecq’s insight, look no further than the response of Berlin LGBT leaders and others to last week’s terror attack on that city’s Pride parade, which left one dead and 29 injured. The suspect, Abdul Ballout, died in a [shootout with police](https://apnews.com/article/germany-berlin-lgbtq-pride-parade-van-ramming-3ba5e3f1becffd0da5f47b29aa08da9d).

It emerged that Ballout, 21, was the German-born son of a Muslim migrant from Lebanon. He had earlier traveled to Lebanon intending to [join ISIS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10328), but was arrested by Lebanese authorities, served a three-month prison sentence, and returned to Germany.

This past May, a Berlin juvenile court convicted Ballout of planning terrorist violence and spreading ISIS propaganda on social media. The court handed down a suspended sentence, leaving him free to drive a car into Pride march goers.

And yet, at a public Berlin vigil for the victims, a young LGBT activist [told the crowd](https://x.com/visegrad24/status/2081502495684087877) that she “had hoped that the attacker was not a foreigner, but a white Christian person.” A reporter for NTV, a popular TV and online news channel, [told viewers](https://www.thefp.com/p/douglas-murray-europe-spins-another-islamist-attack) that “the queer community naturally feels threatened, above all, by right-wing forces.”

Meanwhile, at a press conference, organizers of Berlin Pride told reporters in a statement, “We do not want this deed to be used for political ends. We don’t want any groups to be placed under suspicion for this. People are trying to divide our society and set some people against others. We will not allow this.”

But that’s the thing: Abdul Ballout, like all these Islamist attackers, commit their deeds *precisely* as political and religious acts! The unwillingness of European progressives to see what is right under their noses is infuriating. Even as many German Catholic bishops [work tirelessly to liberalize](https://www.newwaysministry.org/2026/04/15/german-bishop-calls-for-lgbtq-equality-throughout-the-church/) the rapidly declining Church and make it more pro-LGBT, useful idiots like that young mourner insist that the true enemies are the villains they wish for, not the ones they actually have.

Meanwhile, more and more gay Germans are [supporting the Alternative for Germany](https://www.the-berliner.com/berlin/afd-queer-votes-lbgtq-alice-weidel-far-right/) (AfD) party, which is led by Alice Weidel, an out and partnered lesbian. AfD, widely condemned by German establishment figures as “far right,” maintains a hard line on migration, and vows to deport illegals. Despite the party’s platform calling for privileging traditional families, at least some gay Germans prefer a party that disapproves, at least in theory, of their marriages, to political parties soft on Islamists who want them dead.

On Thursday, Weidel [posted footage on X](https://x.com/Alice_Weidel/status/2082820857248858583) of the migrants coming ashore in Ceuta, commenting that their “presumed destination is likely Germany. Borders must now be closed and rejections rigorously enforced. 2015 must not repeat itself!” By “2015,” she means the million-man migration into Europe set off by then-Chancellor Angela Merkel’s infamous declaration, “Wir schaffen das!” (“We can do this!”).

Meanwhile, as authorities in Ceuta struggled to cope with the emergency situation, an American expatriate friend sitting at a cafe on a tourist square in Budapest texted me a short dispatch from post-Orbán Hungary. Shaken, she said a dark-skinned man, who looked like a Muslim migrant or asylum seeker,had just approached her, pointed at her vagina, “grabbed his crotch \[and\] gave me a lascivious licking motion.”

She yelled at him to go away, but he remained there, “licking, grabbing himself,” and making obscene gestures.

“That’s Budapest now, I guess,” she concluded. “How many of these reprobates are in this country?”

Nobody knows, but with a new, EU-friendly government in power, the future is uncertain. The government of Prime Minister Péter Magyar has promised to hold firm on Hungary’s hardline border policies, but has [also signaled openness](https://www.hungarianconservative.com/articles/politics/hungary-new-government-accused-of-betrayal-over-migration-policy-shift/) to the EU’s demand that it accept at least some asylum seekers. Since 2024, Hungary has had to pay a one-million euro ($1.15M) daily fine for defying a European court ruling ordering the country to loosen border restrictions.

So far, the Magyar government has been trying to stick to its campaign promises, but despite being thrilled to no longer have to deal with a bumptious “MAGA-style leader,” EU officials have been holding firm: Hungary will be squeezed until it learns to welcome migrants, like all good Europeans should.

Europe’s rivers may be running dry this summer, but given the suicidal empathy of Western European politicians, judges, and other elites, the flow through the “privileged witnesses of hope” pipeline seems unstoppable.
