From JD Vance to two-thirds of the Supreme Court, Catholicism is a real force among American conservatives, and the religion is attracting new recruits on the right. Journalist Nate Weisberg watched his friend from college convert, joining a burgeoning group of new, young Catholics working in right-wing politics. Linda chats with Nate about his *New York Magazine* piece, “ [The Young Catholic Elite Poised to Take Over MAGA](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/the-young-catholic-elite-poised-to-take-over-maga.html),” and asks why an old faith is newly influential.
